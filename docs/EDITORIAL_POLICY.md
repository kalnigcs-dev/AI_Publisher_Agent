# AI Publisher - Editorial Policy & Content Standards

## 1. Editorial Principles

1. **People-First Helpful Content:**
   - Write to satisfy human search intent, not search engine algorithms.
   - Articles must provide actionable, comprehensive, and original synthesis.
   - Every article must answer the reader's question thoroughly and efficiently.

2. **No Hallucinations or Fabricated Claims:**
   - Every claim of fact, measurement, statistic, historical event, or technical specification must cite a source collected during the research phase.
   - Do not invent quotes, case studies, personal testimonials, or fictitious experts.

3. **Restricted & Off-Limits Niches (YMYL):**
   The autonomous publishing agent is strictly forbidden from publishing unreviewed content on:
   - Medical advice, diagnosis, treatments, or pharmaceuticals.
   - Legal counsel, statutory interpretation, or litigation strategy.
   - Financial advice, stock recommendations, tax filing advice, or cryptocurrency speculation.
   - Breaking news or unverified developing incidents.

4. **Style & Structure:**
   - Clear, hierarchy-compliant HTML headers (`h2`, `h3`, no skipped levels).
   - Clean meta descriptions (140-160 characters).
   - Punchy introductions and clear, structured summaries/key takeaways.
   - Descriptive anchor text for all internal and external links.

5. **Spam & Policy Compliance:**
   - Strict adherence to Google Search Spam Policies.
   - No doorway pages, scraped content, or thin affiliate landing pages.
